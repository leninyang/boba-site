Boba privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Boba is a personal desktop assistant used only by its owner, Lenin Yang. This page explains how it handles Google data.

What Boba accesses

With the owner's permission, Boba connects to his own Gmail and Google Calendar using these Google scopes:

  • gmail.readonly: read email
  • gmail.compose: create drafts and send email
  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly: see the list of calendars
  • calendar.events: read, create, change and delete calendar events

Where the data goes

  • Gmail and Calendar data is accessed only on the owner's Mac.
  • Sign-in tokens stay in Boba's encrypted vault on that Mac.
  • Nothing is sent to any server run by Boba. There is no Boba server.
  • No data is shared with or sold to anyone.
